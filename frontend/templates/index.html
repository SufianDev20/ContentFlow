    Questions

    What can I paste?

    Raw text, or the URL of a public article. For a URL, ContentFlow fetches the page and pulls out the article text before writing anything.

    Which models does it use?

    Requests go to OpenRouter first. If that provider fails, the same request is sent to Gemini. You can choose the model for each job.

    How long does a job take?

    Outputs are generated while you wait, so a long article can take a while. Every output shows its own latency and tokens per second.

    What does the quality score mean?

    It is a 0 to 100 check against the rules of each format, such as numbered tweets under 280 characters or a LinkedIn post of the right length. It is not a judgment of taste.

    Is my content private?

    Your jobs and outputs belong to your account and are only visible when you are signed in. The text is sent to the model provider to be rewritten.

    Is it safe to submit a URL?

    The fetcher only follows http and https links, and refuses localhost, private network addresses and cloud metadata endpoints.

    Is there an API?

    Yes. The same endpoints the app uses are documented with Swagger and Redoc, and the OpenAPI schema is public.
